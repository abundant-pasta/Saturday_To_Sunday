## Weekly QA Checklist

- Every link includes `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`.
- School spotlight links include `school=<SchoolName>`.
- Theme links include `theme=<theme_key>`.
- Links land on the correct sport page.
- `/admin/growth` shows campaign landings after a test click.
- At least one campaign produced measurable lift in starts, shares, claims, or Survival joins.
